How Nova works

Nova searches a built-in set of notes based on your eight-page study guide, then checks any extra facts you have added. It can explain those notes, but it does not have ChatGPT-level open-ended reasoning.

Teach Nova

What “training” means here: this creates a personal, searchable knowledge base saved in your browser. It does not retrain a large neural-network model or send your notes to a server. You can export the knowledge as a backup or dataset.
